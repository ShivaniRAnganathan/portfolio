Welcome back, fellow product adventurers! If you’ve been following my “Chapter and Verse: A Product Manager’s Odyssey” series, you know we’re diving deep into Austin Kleon’s “Show Your Work” faster than a developer deploys to production. Today’s episode? Chapter 3: “Find Your Voice.”

Spoiler alert: It’s not about taking singing lessons to nail those product pitch presentations (although that wouldn’t hurt).Also I got lost among the sweetboxes of Diwali.

The Echo Chamber of Product Management

You know that moment when you catch yourself saying “leverage synergies” or “paradigm shift” in a meeting and wonder, “Who am I becoming?” Yeah, that was me six months ago at Ketto. I was speaking fluent Product Manager™️, but something felt off. I was an echo, not a voice.
