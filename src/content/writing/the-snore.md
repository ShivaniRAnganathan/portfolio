But it’s the ambient, background, barely-noticed sounds that hit the hardest. The shuffle of slippers. The way they cleared their throat before speaking. The hiccup in their laughter. The way they existed in noise.

And we let that go first. Always. Because sound is slippery. It doesn’t stay. Not unless we trap it.

Which brings me back to the Spotify snores.

Forty thousand people aren’t just looking for white noise. They’re looking for someone. Or something. Something lost, something almost forgotten, something that lives in frequencies and vibrations instead of pixels and text.
