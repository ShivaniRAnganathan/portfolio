Chapter 1 of “Show Your Work” and How It’s Transforming My Product Playground at Ketto

Move over, lone wolf product managers! There’s a new pack in town, and it’s called a “scenius.” No, it’s not a rare species of PM that can understand developers’ jokes. It’s something far more powerful, and I’m on a mission to find mine.

Welcome to “Chapter and Verse: A Product Manager’s Odyssey,” where I’m deep-diving into Austin Kleon’s “Show Your Work” faster than a sprint planning session on Red Bull. Why? Well, let’s just say my product roadmap was starting to look more like a product cul-de-sac.( Shout out to Gilmore Girl’s please ❤️️)
