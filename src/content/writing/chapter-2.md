From Scenius to Amateur Hour: A Ketto Case Study

Remember our “Genius Bar” from last week? Well, it’s gotten an upgrade. We now call it the “Amateur Hour” (trademark pending; yes, we serve drinks). The rules? Everyone must approach each problem as if they know nothing about it.

Here’s how it played out:

Our UX designer pretended she’d never seen our app before. Result? She spotted 5 UX issues we’d all become blind to.

Our data analyst approached our metrics as if he’d just learned what numbers were. He questioned why we track certain KPIs, leading to a complete revamp of our analytics dashboard.

I pretended I didn’t know what crowdfunding was.
