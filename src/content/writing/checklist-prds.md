2. Rapid Iteration on User Feedback 🚀

Another big win? Flexibility. The checklist PRD let us pivot much more quickly based on user feedback. While developing the “Fundraiser Updates” feature, we got key feedback from some of our most active fundraisers mid-development. Normally, I’d have had to rewrite large chunks of the PRD to incorporate that feedback, which, honestly, would have probably led to us ignoring it.

But with the checklist PRD? We just added a new item: “Add video update option based on user feedback.” That tiny addition kicked off a series of discussions, and suddenly, we were building a way more engaging feature.
