I spoke less, but I meant more. I dropped the performative edge. I stopped overthinking sentence structure and started listening to tone. Pilani taught me to feel in multiple tongues. It was the birthplace of stillness in my speech.

Cue: Bombay.

Bombay didn’t ask me to speak. It dared me. The city is fast. It snaps. It doesn’t wait for your words to line up in single file. You either keep up or get drowned in the honking. Here, language is style. Attitude. Sass. A one-liner on Marine Drive. A sarcastic quip in the middle of a fight. My English sharpened, my Hindi got bolder, and even my Tamil learned to roll its eyes. Bombay taught me speed. Taught me sass. Taught me how to end a sentence with a smirk.
