sections:
  - heading: Context
    paragraphs:
      - primary: Ketto is a crowdfunding platform focused on healthcare causes. My brief was to grow gross merchandise value.
  - heading: The problem
    paragraphs:
      - primary: Data suggested many donors were willing to give more but were not being prompted. The flow was one cause, one transaction. Donor feedback pointed to three gaps. They didn't see the range of causes, they didn't want to repeat checkout, and the urge to give faded quickly.
  - heading: What I believed
    items:
      - primary: Giving is emotional, and the gap between trigger and action decides the amount. Every extra step between "I want to help" and "done" costs money.
      - primary: Repeating a payment flow is a fixed cost donors pay per cause. Remove it and the marginal cause becomes cheap to add.
      - primary: Relevance matters. Suggestions have to match what a donor has cared about before, or they read as noise.
  - heading: Options, and what I didn't do
    items:
      - primary: A cart-style checkout for several causes, instead of separate follow-up asks after each donation. It followed a familiar commerce pattern.
      - primary: Prompts during the donation stayed non-intrusive. That traded some upsell reach for a lower risk of annoying donors mid-payment.
        pending: true
      - primary: A phased rollout with an A/B test, not a big-bang release.
  - heading: Decision, and how it was tested
    items:
      - primary: I ran donor surveys, behavioural analytics, and a review of how similar platforms handled multiple donations.
      - primary: With design, I worked on the prompts and the cart flow. With engineering, I scoped processing several donations in one secure transaction.
      - primary: Personalized cause suggestions were based on past activity and interests.
      - primary: I wrote the requirement. The first PRD ran to 20 pages, which later led me to switch to checklist PRDs.
      - primary: I set up dashboards for average donation size, causes per session and drop-off, and A/B tested before the full launch.
