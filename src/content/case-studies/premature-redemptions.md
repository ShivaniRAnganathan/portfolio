sections:
  - heading: Context
    paragraphs:
      - primary: A redemption is the moment an investor's assets leave the platform. I owned the redemption journey, known internally as Smart Redemption, along with Wealth Dashboard and Transaction History.
  - heading: The problem
    items:
      - primary: Investors confirmed redemptions without seeing exit load or capital gains tax. The release notes lead with awareness of charges before confirming.
      - primary: Retention outreach only happened after the request. Sales called people who had chosen a loan against mutual funds and still redeemed. Those calls were within two hours, to try to retain the assets. Common reasons were that a goal had been reached, or that they wanted the money out.
  - heading: What I believed
    items:
      - primary: A redemption someone later regrets usually comes from something they didn't know. The tax, the exit load, or a cheaper alternative. It rarely comes from a changed goal.
        pending: true
      - primary: The cheapest place to step in is the redemption screen, before the investor confirms. A call afterwards costs more.
        pending: true
      - primary: Investors who have reached their goal should leave freely. Inform, don't block.
        pending: true
  - heading: Options, and what I didn't do
    items:
      - primary: I stepped in on the redemption screen rather than waiting for a call after the request.
        pending: true
      - primary: I informed investors who had reached their goal. I did not block the exit.
        pending: true
      - primary: NRI accounts, segregated folios, and HUF or corporate cases were deferred to later releases.
  - heading: Decision, and how it was tested
    items:
      - primary: A loan against mutual funds, offered inside the redemption flow, with the lending partner. I shared the redemption screens and worked on the copy. A partner review of the first phase was scheduled for 21 August 2025.
      - primary: A tax and exit-load breakdown before confirmation. I ran walkthroughs and design reviews with engineering, design and QA through August and September 2025. The release shows exit load, short-term capital gains and long-term capital gains, phased across web, Android and iOS. I signed off user-acceptance testing on 3 October 2025.
      - primary: A clearer path to a person. I redesigned the weekly portfolio mailer and gave the Let's Talk section much more prominence. It rolled out on 13 September 2025. Pre-redemption alerts send the client's reason to their advisor.
      - primary: I collected advisor-reported calculation issues in October 2025, and planned a liquid-fund switcher and "wait to save tax" messaging for November and December 2025.
  - heading: Result
    items:
      - primary: Live on web, Android and iOS. Partners were briefed on 6 October 2025.
      - primary: 18% fewer premature exits, and 12% higher long-term retention value, from ML-based churn prediction, behavioural nudges and contextual decision flows. Baseline, cohort, time window and measurement method are not stated.
